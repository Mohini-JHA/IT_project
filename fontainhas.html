PANAJI • LATIN QUARTER

Fontainhas

Colourful streets, old houses and the living culture of Panaji.

HISTORY & ARCHITECTURE

A Walk Through Old Panaji

Fontainhas is Panaji's historic Latin Quarter, known for narrow lanes and Portuguese-influenced houses with colourful façades, tiled roofs, balconies and decorative windows.

The neighbourhood developed as Panaji grew in importance as Goa's administrative capital. Its architecture reflects the meeting of local building traditions with Portuguese influences.

Today, Fontainhas is a living neighbourhood. Walk quietly, respect residents' privacy and avoid blocking doorways while taking photographs.

Visitor Information

Location: Panaji, Goa

Suggested visit: 1–2 hours

Best time: Morning or late afternoon

Timings: Public streets can be explored respectfully during daylight.

Entry: Public lanes are generally walkable; respect private property.
