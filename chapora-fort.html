CHAPORA • COASTAL HISTORY

Chapora Fort

Hilltop ruins, dramatic views and a glimpse into Goa's past.

HISTORY & LANDSCAPE

History Above the Coast

Chapora Fort stands on a hill overlooking the Chapora River and the Arabian Sea. The site had earlier fortifications, while the Portuguese built the surviving fort in 1717.

Its position gave defenders a wide view of the surrounding waterways and coastline. Today, much of the fort is in ruins, and its hilltop setting attracts visitors for the panoramic scenery.

The uphill walk can be uneven, so suitable footwear is recommended.

Visitor Information

Location: Chapora, near Vagator

Suggested visit: 45–90 minutes

Best time: Early morning or late afternoon

Timings: Check local access conditions.

Entry: Verify current rules before visiting.
