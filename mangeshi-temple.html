PRIOL • GOAN SPIRITUAL HERITAGE

Mangeshi Temple

A celebrated temple surrounded by tradition and devotion.

HISTORY & TRADITION

A Sacred Goan Landmark

Shri Mangeshi Temple, in Priol, is an important Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. The temple's traditions are connected with Goa's long-standing religious and cultural history.

The community relocated the deity's worship site during the period of Portuguese expansion, and the present temple complex developed in Priol. Its architecture features a distinctive deepastambha, courtyards and a traditional Goan temple layout.

Visitors should dress respectfully and observe temple rules regarding photography and entry.

Visitor Information

Location: Priol, Ponda region

Suggested visit: 30–60 minutes

Best time: Morning, outside busy rituals

Timings: Confirm with the temple.

Entry: Follow local temple guidelines.
