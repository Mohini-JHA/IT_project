REIS MAGOS • MANDOVI RIVER

Reis Magos Fort

A restored riverside fort with centuries of stories.

HISTORY & CULTURE

Guardian of the River

Reis Magos Fort stands on the northern bank of the Mandovi River. Its original fortification dates to 1551, and it was later expanded and used for military and strategic purposes.

Over time, the site served different roles, including a defensive position and other institutional uses. Restoration has helped preserve the structure as a cultural attraction.

The location offers views across the river towards Panaji and provides insight into Goa's coastal history.

Visitor Information

Location: Reis Magos, Bardez

Suggested visit: 45–90 minutes
