DONA PAULA • COASTAL HERITAGE

Cabo Raj Bhavan

A historic residence on a scenic headland overlooking the sea.

HISTORY & PLACE

A Historic Coastal Residence

Cabo Raj Bhavan stands on the Cabo headland near Dona Paula, overlooking the meeting of coastal waters near the Mandovi and Zuari river mouths.

The site has a long association with Portuguese-era administration and later became the official residence of the Governor of Goa. Its location and buildings reflect the area's political and coastal history.

Because it is an official government residence, public access may be restricted. Visitors should not assume the buildings or grounds are open for general sightseeing.

Visitor Information

Location: Cabo, Dona Paula area

Suggested visit: Allow time for nearby viewpoints

Best time: Daylight hours

Access: Verify current public access before travelling.

Note: Respect security restrictions and signage.
