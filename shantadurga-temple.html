FATORPA • TEMPLE HERITAGE

Shantadurga Temple

A significant temple and a centre of Goan religious traditions.

HISTORY & TRADITION

A Place of Peace and Devotion

Shri Shantadurga Temple at Fatorpa is an important place of worship dedicated to Goddess Shantadurga. The goddess is revered as a symbol of peace and harmony.

The temple's history reflects the resilience of Goan Hindu communities and the development of local religious traditions. Its architecture and festivals remain an important part of the region's cultural life.

Visitors are encouraged to dress modestly, respect worshippers and follow the temple's current photography and entry rules.

Visitor Information

Location: Fatorpa, South Goa

Suggested visit: 30–60 minutes

Best time: Morning or outside major festivals

Timings: Confirm locally.

Entry: Follow temple guidelines.
