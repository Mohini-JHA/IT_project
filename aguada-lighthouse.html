FORT AGUADA • MARITIME HISTORY

Aguada Lighthouse

A coastal landmark linked with navigation and Goa's seafaring past.

HISTORY & NAVIGATION

Guiding Ships Along the Coast

The historic lighthouse at Fort Aguada is associated with Goa's maritime heritage. An earlier lighthouse at the fort dates to the nineteenth century and helped signal the coastline to ships.

The wider Aguada complex was an important Portuguese defensive and water-supply site. Its location demonstrates how navigation, trade and coastal defence shaped the region's history.

Access to the lighthouse itself may be restricted. Visitors should follow current site instructions and stay within permitted areas.

Visitor Information

Location: Fort Aguada area, Candolim

Suggested visit: 30–60 minutes

Best time: Morning or late afternoon

Timings: Confirm access and schedules locally.

Entry: Check whether the lighthouse area is open.
