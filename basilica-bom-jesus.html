HISTORY & ARCHITECTURE

A Remarkable Historic Basilica

The Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa is one of the region's best-known churches. Consecrated in 1605, it is an important example of Baroque architecture and forms part of the Churches and Convents of Goa UNESCO World Heritage property.

The basilica is associated with St. Francis Xavier, whose relics are preserved here. Its historic interiors and artistic details make it an important religious and cultural destination.

Visitors should remember that this is an active place of worship and follow local guidance.

Visitor Information
