A LITTLE PLANNING GOES A LONG WAY

Your Goa Travel Guide

Useful tips for a safe, relaxed and memorable trip.

TRAVEL LIKE A LOCAL

Everything You Should Know

Simple ideas to help you plan your journey around Goa.

01 / WEATHER

Best Time to Visit

November to February is popular for comfortable outdoor sightseeing. Monsoon months bring lush scenery but may affect travel plans.

02 / TRANSPORT

Getting Around

Compare taxi, bus and rental options. Confirm fares, rental terms and licence requirements before travelling.

03 / PACKING

What to Carry

Pack sunscreen, a hat, water, comfortable shoes, light clothing and a reusable bag.

04 / CULTURE

Respect Local Traditions

Dress respectfully at religious sites, follow photography rules and respect local customs.

05 / FOOD

Try Goan Cuisine

Explore local thalis, Goan fish curry, poi bread and bebinca. Ask about ingredients if you have allergies.

06 / SAFETY

Beach Safety

Follow lifeguard flags, avoid rough seas, keep valuables secure and respect restricted areas.

07 / HERITAGE

Check Timings

Monuments, museums and places of worship may have different hours or special closures. Verify before visiting.

08 / ESSENTIALS

Stay Prepared

Keep emergency contacts available, carry identification securely and have a backup payment option.

09 / RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Leave No Trace

Use bins, reduce plastic waste, support local businesses and avoid disturbing wildlife.

10 / ITINERARY

Plan Your Days

Group nearby sights together and allow extra time for traffic, meals and unexpected weather.

Suggested 3-day Goa itinerary

Day 1: Old Goa churches and Panaji's Fontainhas heritage quarter.

Day 2: Fort Aguada, Reis Magos and nearby coastal scenery.

Day 3: Explore South Goa, try local food and enjoy a relaxed beach visit.

This is a sample itinerary. Verify opening hours, transport and local conditions before travelling.
