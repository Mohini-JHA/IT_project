Se Cathedral

A grand cathedral reflecting Goa's historic religious heritage.

HISTORY & ARCHITECTURE

A Monumental Cathedral

Se Cathedral in Old Goa was built during Portuguese rule and dedicated to St. Catherine of Alexandria. Construction began in the sixteenth century, and the cathedral was consecrated in 1640.

Its scale, classical design and historic interiors make it one of the most significant churches in Goa. It is included in the UNESCO-listed Churches and Convents of Goa.

The cathedral remains a place of worship and an important heritage landmark.
