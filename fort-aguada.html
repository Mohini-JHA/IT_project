HISTORIC GOA • CANDOLIM

Fort Aguada

A coastal fortress with stories of maritime history and Portuguese Goa.

HISTORY & ARCHITECTURE

A Fortress Facing the Arabian Sea

Fort Aguada was built by the Portuguese in the early seventeenth century to help defend the coastline and protect shipping approaching Goa. Its strategic location near the mouth of the Mandovi River made it an important part of the region's coastal defences.

The fort is associated with a freshwater storage system that supplied water to ships. The name Aguada is linked to the Portuguese word for water.

Today, the fort is a popular historic attraction where visitors can explore its surviving structures and enjoy views of the Arabian Sea.

Visitor Information

Location: Sinquerim, Candolim, North Goa

Suggested duration: 45–90 minutes

Best time: Morning or late afternoon

Timings: Check current official/local timings before visiting.

Entry fee: Verify current rules and any charges before your visit.

Travel Tip

Wear comfortable footwear, carry water and avoid restricted or unsafe edges. Opening hours and access may change.
